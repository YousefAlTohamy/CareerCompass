# Defense Presentation Outline

This outline is designed for a 10-15 minute graduation defense. Use careful
wording: extracted, predicted, estimated, and available imported jobs. Do not
say guaranteed, perfect, global coverage, or final accuracy unless backed by
measured results.

## 1. Title Slide

- Main message: CareerCompass is a graduation project for CV-based career
  guidance and job recommendation.
- What to show: project title, team, supervisor, faculty/department, and date.
- What not to overclaim: do not describe it as a production hiring platform.

## 2. Problem

- Main message: students need help connecting CV skills to realistic job paths.
- What to show: short scenario showing unstructured CVs and varied job
  requirements.
- What not to overclaim: do not imply the system replaces advisors or hiring
  decisions.

## 3. Objectives

- Main message: parse CVs, extract skills, recommend jobs, show gaps, and
  provide admin diagnostics.
- What to show: 4-5 concise objectives.
- What not to overclaim: do not promise perfect matching or guaranteed jobs.

## 4. Proposed Solution

- Main message: CareerCompass combines CV analysis, normalized skills, imported
  jobs, recommendations, and explainable gap analysis.
- What to show: simple product flow from CV upload to saved opportunity.
- What not to overclaim: avoid "global market coverage"; use "available
  imported jobs".

## 5. System Architecture

- Main message: the project demonstrates a Docker-first distributed
  architecture.
- What to show: architecture diagram with React, Laravel, Python AI analyzer,
  job miner, MySQL, queues, storage, and monitoring.
- What not to overclaim: do not frame local Docker demo as production cloud
  deployment.

## 6. CV Analysis Pipeline

- Main message: uploaded CVs are processed into structured profile signals.
- What to show: upload sequence and example extracted skills/status.
- What not to overclaim: avoid "perfect understanding"; say extracted,
  predicted, or partial where appropriate.

## 7. Skill Normalization

- Main message: normalized skills make matching and gap analysis more reliable.
- What to show: examples such as JS to JavaScript and React.js to React.
- What not to overclaim: do not imply all ambiguous skills are solved.

## 8. Job Recommendation And Gap Analysis

- Main message: matching compares user skills/profile context with job
  requirements and explains gaps.
- What to show: matched skills, missing skills, estimated score, and suggested
  improvements.
- What not to overclaim: do not call the match score a hiring probability.

## 9. Scraping And Market Data

- Main message: the system imports jobs from reliable demo/API sources and
  classifies public-source reliability honestly.
- What to show: source matrix and source diagnostics.
- What not to overclaim: do not promise live worldwide jobs or bypass blocked
  sites.

## 10. Admin And Diagnostics

- Main message: admin pages make system data and source status visible for the
  graduation demo.
- What to show: admin dashboard, source diagnostics, and health/status page.
- What not to overclaim: do not present sample or cached values as measured
  live infrastructure telemetry.

## 11. Evaluation Framework

- Main message: the project includes scripts and guides for honest academic
  evaluation.
- What to show: evaluation folder, metrics, sample/template labels, and command
  examples.
- What not to overclaim: do not claim final accuracy unless final labeled data
  was evaluated.

## 12. Demo Flow

- Main message: the live demo follows the student and admin paths end to end.
- What to show: login, CV upload, skills, jobs, gap analysis, tracker, admin
  diagnostics, and status checks.
- What not to overclaim: do not hide degraded external sources; explain them.

## 13. Limitations

- Main message: the project is honest about academic scope and known limits.
- What to show: small dataset, CV variability, Arabic/mixed-language testing,
  external source blocking, and guidance-only matching.
- What not to overclaim: do not call limitations solved unless demonstrated.

## 14. Future Work

- Main message: the system can continue into stronger research and
  productization after graduation.
- What to show: larger dataset, multilingual support, skill ontology, official
  job APIs, explainability, mobile app, and production deployment.
- What not to overclaim: keep production deployment as future work.

## 15. Conclusion

- Main message: CareerCompass demonstrates architecture, AI/NLP, matching,
  scraping diagnostics, testing, and demo readiness.
- What to show: final summary of contributions and the live-demo achievement.
- What not to overclaim: end with confidence in the graduation system, not
  production readiness claims.
